Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This Privacy Policy explains how GoalHub EU ("we", "us") collects and uses information when you visit our website.

1. Information we collect

  • Usage data: pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser type, collected via analytics.
  • Preferences: your selected country and consent choice, stored locally in your browser.
  • We do not require accounts and do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

2. Cookies & advertising

We use cookies for analytics and to display advertising. We work with third-party vendors, including Google, who use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising via Google Ads Settings.
  • For more options, visit youronlinechoices.eu (EU) or aboutads.info.

3. Your consent (EU/UK)

When you first visit, we ask for your consent before loading personalised advertising and non-essential cookies. You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing your browser storage for this site.

4. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, or erase your personal data and to object to processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

5. Third-party links

Our "Where to Watch" links and embedded videos point to third-party services (broadcasters, YouTube). Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email privacy@goalhub.example.
